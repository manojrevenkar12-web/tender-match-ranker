# 0002 Log every position; serve a few lots shuffled

The logs are written by the ranking that was live. A firm at position 0 is invited far more often than the same
firm at position 15, so logged outcomes mix "good firm" with "shown first". To separate them the platform
serves a small share of lots (5–8 %) from a shuffled shortlist and logs which lots those were: there, position is
independent of the firm, and the invitation rate by position is the examination curve. On the simulated market
the measured curve is within 0.01 of the true one. The cost is a few lots a month with a slightly worse order.
