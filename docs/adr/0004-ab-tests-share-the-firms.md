# 0004 A/B tests share the firms: read the estimate as a lower bound

Buyers in both arms invite from the same firms, so a ranker that spreads invitations also relieves firms the
control arm asks, and the control arm's floods reach the treatment's firms. The buyer-split A/B estimate on the
simulated market is about a fifth below the true effect of switching every buyer, and its 95 % interval misses
the true effect. Reported as is; a switchback by region and week is the design to use when the size of the
effect matters, not only its sign (CUPED helps little: buyers' earlier months predict little of a lot's bids).
